<h3 align="center">The company behind <a href="https://daycarelogix.com">DaycareLogix</a>, <a href="https://exolvra.ai">Exolvra</a>, and <a href="https://steddyzen.com">SteddyZen</a>.</h3>

---

Care on one side. Circuits on the other. EvolvLabs is an independent software company in Sykesville, Maryland. We build an operations platform for daycares, a workplace for AI agents, and a planner designed for ADHD. Three very different tools, all built the same careful way.

Our specialty is AI and software architecture: multi-agent systems, cloud-native platforms, and the compliance plumbing that keeps regulated work honest.

## What we build

### DaycareLogix · [daycarelogix.com](https://daycarelogix.com)

Operations software for childcare centers and adult day programs. Enrollment, attendance, daily care logs, CACFP meal counts, billing, and payroll all live on a single participant record. Staff record things as they happen, so nobody has to reconstruct the month later. In production today.

### Exolvra · [exolvra.ai](https://exolvra.ai)

The right software for your agents. Exolvra gives AI agents a project board instead of a chat box: agents break down projects, work in teams, and use real tools across dozens of integrations, while guardrails check their work before it reaches you. Self-hosted, so your data stays on your network. Waitlist open.

### SteddyZen · [steddyzen.com](https://steddyzen.com)

A calm planner built for ADHD. It breaks work into next steps that are easy to start, keeps routines flexible, and shows progress without piling on pressure. In development, coming to desktop and mobile.

More products are in the works. [evolvlabs.ai](https://evolvlabs.ai) always has the current list.

## How we build

We work in places where mistakes are expensive: children's records, health information, federal reimbursement funds, and AI agents with access to production systems. So compliance is architecture, not an afterthought. Tenant isolation, role-based access, and server-side enforcement sit in the foundation of every product. If a rule matters, the software enforces it.

We also write it all down. Our security posture, CSA STAR Registry listings, and CAIQ and AI-CAIQ self-assessments are published in the open at our [Trust Center](https://evolvlabs.ai/trust/), where anyone can read them before ever talking to us. WCAG 2.1 AA is our accessibility floor.

We design, build, and run our products end to end. We don't outsource engineering, we don't sell data, and we don't run ads.
